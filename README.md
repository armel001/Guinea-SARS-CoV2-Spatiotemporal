## Associated Publication

This repository is linked to the following peer-reviewed publication:

Gnimadi T.A.C., Kadio K.J.-J.O., Mathew M.J., Diallo H., Soumah A.K., Keita A.K., Hounmenou C.G., Fernandez-Nuñez N., Vidal N., Guichet E., et al.  
*Genetic Diversity and Spatiotemporal Distribution of SARS-CoV-2 Variants in Guinea: A Meta-Analysis of Sequence Data (2020–2023).*  
Viruses. 2025; 17(2):204.  
https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4915/17/2/204  

### Study Overview

This study describes the genetic diversity and spatiotemporal distribution of SARS-CoV-2 variants circulating in Guinea during the COVID-19 pandemic using publicly available viral genome sequences collected between March 2020 and December 2023.

### Key Findings

- A total of 1,038 SARS-CoV-2 genomes generated in Guinea were analyzed.
- 73.1% of sequences corresponded to Variants of Concern (VOCs), including:
  - Omicron (69.4%)
  - Delta (21.9%)
  - Alpha (6.6%)
  - Eta (2.1%)
- Phylogeographic analysis identified 75 viral importation events into Guinea:
  - 40% from Africa
  - 25.3% from Europe
  - 18.6% from Asia

### Impact

The findings highlight the importance of large-scale genomic surveillance to better understand viral evolution, variant dissemination, and cross-border transmission dynamics in West Africa.
